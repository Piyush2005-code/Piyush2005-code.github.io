IIT Jodhpur · CSE

Engineering AI Systems

I am an undergraduate Computer Science student at IIT Jodhpur focused on AI systems, operating systems, and computer architecture.

My work explores how modern AI workloads interact with hardware and system software — building GPU-accelerated inference pipelines, lightweight operating systems for ML workloads, and distributed AI systems.

I also have a keen passion for design and aesthetics expressed through CAD Modelling in the domain of Robotics.

A systems-focused engineer building where AI infrastructure meets hardware reality.

I am an undergraduate Computer Science student at IIT Jodhpur working at the intersection of AI systems, operating systems, and computer architecture. My work is motivated by one core question: how do we make AI workloads run faster, leaner, and closer to the metal?

I build infrastructure for machine learning — GPU-accelerated inference pipelines, bare-metal unikernels, and distributed AI systems. From a 256KB ARM64 OS to a voice assistant serving 500+ concurrent users, I work across the full hardware-software stack.

Beyond software, I am deeply interested in Robotics and CAD design, having designed quadcopters and high-lift aircraft wings that earned me a place at Inter IIT Tech Meet 14.0.
